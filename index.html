02 / Behind the projects

A little about me.

I’ve always been the kind of person who wants to figure things out. When something doesn’t work, I like understanding why and trying different ways to fix it. Computer science felt like a natural fit for that part of me. I decided to pursue it during my sophomore year of high school, when I started taking coding classes and discovered how much I enjoyed turning that curiosity into something I could build.

That interest led to an opportunity to build a stock sentiment predictor for Amazon. I collected recent news articles, analyzed their polarity and subjectivity, and used Azure Machine Learning Studio to explore whether those signals could help predict stock movement. It was my first experience bringing several tools together to investigate a question of my own.

Since then, I’ve worked with languages including Python, Java, JavaScript, and C through individual and team projects. What keeps me interested is having a problem in front of me and finding ways to solve it. Building software gives me a way to turn those ideas into something I can test and improve.

That willingness to figure things out has been part of my life outside of coding, too. Moving from Brazil to Miami in 2017 meant adjusting to a new environment and learning as I went. Today, I speak Portuguese, Spanish, and English. When I’m taking a break from school or projects, I enjoy following Formula 1, reading, and puzzles.
